Customer Support FAQ

1.  What is the refund policy?
    Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase if the
    product is unused.

2.  How can I track my order?
    You can track your order using the tracking link sent to your
    registered email.

3.  How do I cancel my order?
    Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours from the time of purchase.

4.  What are the delivery charges?
    Delivery charges depend on your location and will be shown at
    checkout.

5.  How long does delivery take?
    Standard delivery usually takes 3–5 business days.

6.  What should I do if I receive a damaged product?
    Please contact customer support within 48 hours with photos of the
    damaged item.

7.  How can I change my shipping address?
    You can update your address from the Orders section before the order
    is shipped.

8.  Do you provide customer support 24/7?
    Yes, our customer support team is available 24/7 via chat and email.

9.  How can I contact customer support?
    You can contact us through live chat, email, or our toll‑free
    number.

10. Do you offer product warranty?
    Yes, most products come with a 1‑year manufacturer warranty.
